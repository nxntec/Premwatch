Sunglasses store

04 · The bench

If it has stopped, bring it in.

Diagnostic repair, not a guess from across the counter. The case comes open, the movement gets a look, and you hear what it needs — a battery, a strap, ordinary service, or a plain no when the part isn’t one we keep.

  • Diagnosis before a promise
  • Batteries, straps, and routine service
  • An honest answer when a part isn’t in stock
